    Everyword Bagel

    All the tiles start face down in one big bagel. Take turns flipping letters into the middle, claim words, and steal your friends' words by adding letters. No mercy.

    Everyword Bagel

    How to play

    Every tile starts face down in the outer ring of the bagel. The middle is the floor: only tiles that have been flipped face up there can be used. There are no turns for claiming — the server is the referee, so the first valid claim wins.

    Reveal

    Only one player may flip a tile at a time, in the order you joined the table. On your turn, tap any face-down tile to turn it face up in the middle. The turn to reveal then passes to the next player. Stuck for letters? Reveal more.

    Claim a word

    Type a word that uses face-up tiles from the middle. The minimum is 4 letters by default (the host can change it to 3–7 in the lobby). The word must be in the NWL2023 Scrabble dictionary. The tiles leave the middle and become yours.

    Steal a word

    Just type a word that keeps every letter of a word already on the table and adds at least one face-up letter from the middle. Rearranging is expected, and you never have to tap the old word first: the steal is detected as you type. You can extend anyone's word, including your own.

    CATS + H → CHATS

    Their word disappears and yours takes its place. A successful steal also gives you the next reveal, so you can flip another tile before the rotation continues. You can rebuild your own words the same way, and steal a word that was itself stolen; the table gets delightfully petty.

    Scoring

    Your score is the total number of letters in all your words. The round ends automatically when every tile has been used, or when the host calls time. Most letters wins. Ties share the bagel.

    Good to know

    • Face-down letters are secret. They are never sent to the browser until revealed.
    • Words are checked against the bundled NWL2023 Scrabble list.
    • Duplicate words in different players' piles are allowed.
    • Refreshing a tab reconnects you if the browser kept the session.
    • The game state lives on the host's server; restarting the server starts fresh.
